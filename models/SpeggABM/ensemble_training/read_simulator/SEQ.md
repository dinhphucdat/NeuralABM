# How the sequencing data are simulated

The ART_454 tool performs the best with a sequence of 150 bp, and the sPEGG simulation models 3 different loci, so we are going to pick point mutations at positions 10, 50, and 100 as reference genotypes.

We will copy the first 150 bp of an actual ecoli DNA sequence as a reference genome, and then go ahead and mutate from there.
